Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

Flex Miles has no account, no ads and no tracking for advertising. Everything you record stays on your iPhone. We, the makers of the app, never receive your location, shifts, places or money.

What the app records, and where it's kept

  • Your location, only while a shift is running, to count miles and notice when you reach your station or home.
  • Your shifts: times, miles and the parts of each shift.
  • Places you save: home and stations.
  • Money you type in: block pay, tips, tolls, parking, fill-ups, your car and gas price.

All of this is saved in files on your iPhone. It is included in your iPhone's own backup if you use iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles.

When the app goes online

  • Address search: what you type to find a home or station address is sent to Apple Maps to show matching places.
  • "Use my current location": when you tap it while saving a home or station, that one spot is sent to Apple to look up its street address.
  • Car lookup: the year, make and model you pick are sent to fueleconomy.gov, the U.S. government's free fuel economy site, to get your car's MPG. Only those car details are sent.

Your shifts, miles and money are never sent. The app sends nothing else anywhere.

Location

Location is used only during a shift: from when you tap Start shift until you arrive home or cancel. You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings, but the app can't count miles without it.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data. We can't: it never leaves your phone except for the lookups above.

Deleting your data

Delete a shift by swiping it left. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on this phone.

Children

Flex Miles is made for adult delivery drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page with a new date.
