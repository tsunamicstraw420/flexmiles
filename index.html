Flex Miles support

Flex Miles counts the miles and time of every delivery shift, and shows what each block really earned after gas.

How a shift works

  1. Start shift when you leave home. The app counts your drive to the station.
  2. It notices when you stop at a saved station, and when you drive away with packages.
  3. Tap Last drop done after your last delivery.
  4. The shift ends by itself when you stop at home.

Common questions

Why does the app ask for location "Always"?

So miles keep counting while your screen is off or you're in the delivery app. Location is used only while a shift is running.

How is earnings per hour worked out?

Profit divided by the time from leaving the station with packages to your last drop. Profit is your block pay and tips, minus gas, tolls and parking. Gas for the whole shift, including the drive to the station and home, still counts against profit.

How is the gas cost worked out?

Miles ÷ your car's MPG × the gas price. The price comes from your most recent fill-up. The MPG starts as your car's EPA rating. After two full-tank fill-ups with odometer readings, the app uses your real MPG instead.

I work two blocks in one day. What do I do?

After your last drop, tap Going to another block instead of driving home. The first block ends at its last drop. The drive to the next station counts for the next block.

Where can I see my past shifts?

Tap the list button at the top left, any time, even during a shift.

How do I delete a shift?

Swipe it left in the list of shifts.

Is my data shared?

No. There is no account, and everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
